<p>On 21 April 2025, <strong>Ranjana Mahesh</strong> performed Kuchipudi as part of the ceremonial welcome for U.S. Vice President JD Vance during his visit to India at Indira Gandhi International Airport, Terminal 1, in New Delhi.</p>

<p>The presentation featured a <em>Jathiswaram</em>, combining rhythmic precision, graceful movement, and the vibrant energy of Indian classical dance to mark the occasion.</p>
