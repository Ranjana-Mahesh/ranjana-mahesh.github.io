<p>On 13 November 2024, at Ramagya School in Greater Noida, <strong>Ranjana Mahesh</strong> served as a judge for the dance competition <em>Rhapsody</em>.</p>

<p>The programme was centred on the theme <em>Endangered Creativity in the Time of AI</em>, exploring creativity and artistic expression in an era increasingly shaped by artificial intelligence.</p>
