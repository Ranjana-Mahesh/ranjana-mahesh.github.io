<p>On 6 January 2025, at Bharat Mandapam in New Delhi, <strong>Ranjana Mahesh</strong> performed in <em>The Divine Nectar</em>, a Kuchipudi production presented as part of <em>Grameen Bharat Mahotsav</em>.</p>

<p>Organised by the Department of Financial Services, Government of India, the festival celebrated the theme <em>Harvest – Rhythms of Earth</em>, bringing together artistic traditions rooted in India's cultural heritage.</p>
