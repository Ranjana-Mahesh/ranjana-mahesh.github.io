<p>On 23 August 2026, <strong>Ranjana Mahesh</strong> presented a Kuchipudi performance for the Onam celebrations at the Mata Amritanandamayi Math in Trivandrum.</p>

<p>The recital brought together two devotional compositions: <em>Lambodara</em> and <em>Pibare Rama Rasam</em>. Through expressive storytelling and graceful movement, the performance celebrated devotion and the joyful spirit of Onam.</p>
